HIMALCAFE & BAKERY

Café & bakery, Kathmandu Valley · 2024 · Design Studio IV

Type
Café & bakery · commercial
Year
2024
Location
Kathmandu Valley, Nepal
Team
Studio project
Software
AutoCAD · SketchUp · Enscape · Photoshop

Brief

Design a café and bakery as a place of pause: a warm interior of scent, light and human scale set against the Kathmandu Valley, where people stop, gather and reconnect around food.

Concept · a warm pause

The space is imagined as a pause along a mountain journey. Sloping roofs recall Himalayan ridgelines; natural materials, warm tones and open glazing bring the landscape inside, so the building itself feels like shelter after a climb.
