HIMALCAFE & BAKERY

Cafe & bakery, Kathmandu Valley

Type
Cafe & bakery · Commercial
Location
Kathmandu Valley, Nepal
Team
Studio project
Software
AutoCAD · SketchUp · Enscape · Photoshop · AI

Brief

Design a cafe and bakery as a place of pause: a warm interior of scent, light and human scale set against the Kathmandu Valley, where people stop, gather and reconnect around food.

Concept · a warm pause

The space is imagined as a pause along a mountain journey. Sloping roofs recall Himalayan ridgelines; natural materials, warm tones and open glazing bring the landscape inside, so the building itself feels like shelter after a climb.
