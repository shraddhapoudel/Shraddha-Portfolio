About

ABOUT

Shraddha Poudel is an architecture student based in Bhaktapur, designing spaces through culture, care, play and community.

I study architecture because I want to know how a building can hold memory, play, care and everyday life, and then learn to build it.

I'm a Bachelor of Architecture student at Nepal Engineering College (Pokhara University), based in Bhaktapur. Through the design studios I have taken projects from a single idea to a full design: a pre-primary school in Bhaktapur shaped round the protective logic of a rabbit burrow, a cafe in the Kathmandu Valley that is a warm pause on a mountain journey, and साहारा, a rural community centre in Khopasi gathered as a circle of care. Over the year, my work has moved between three scales: the drawing, the detail and the community it serves.

Alongside the studio, I have supported thesis projects and academic work, and I've taken part in exhibition and publication work with Nec-Ingenium, ASA-Exhibition and PRARAMBH. I draw by hand as often as I model: hand rendering and sketching are where I think most clearly about space.

What I'm looking for now: an architecture internship where I can work closely with a design team, on site visits, construction drawings, model-making and visualisation, and learn how architecture gets built, not just imagined.

Position

Architecture, for me, is an exploration of how spaces shape experience, interaction and belonging. I care about buildings that respond to their culture rather than replace it, that make room for play and care, and that serve the community around them.
